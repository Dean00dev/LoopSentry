# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led. Disagreement about detector behaviour is welcome; harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, and publication of private model output are not.

Examples of constructive participation include:

- reproducing a claimed failure with a minimal synthetic fixture;
- separating facts, measurements, and interpretations;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting claims when evidence changes;
- making the project accessible to people with different backgrounds and abilities.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Maintainers should explain moderation decisions when doing so would not expose private or safety-sensitive information.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive information.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially representing the project.
